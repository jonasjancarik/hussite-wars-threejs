# Third-party and source provenance

The rendering graph and high-quality policy in `src/render-pipeline.ts`, clear-afternoon light state in `src/lighting.ts`, camera values in `src/camera.ts`, static batching in `src/static-batching.ts`, focus policy in `src/effects.ts`, painted sky plus lower atmospheric veil in `src/sky.ts`, and the rolling performance tracker plus public renderer-counter reader in `src/performance.ts` are narrow adaptations of the Jonas Jancarik `procedural-worlds` project at commit `bada861a8d5c8cb7275a1b3d6e6a3f4ea4844cf4`. That source checkout was clean when transferred. No runtime import or dependency on the sibling checkout remains.

The `assets/models/procedural-worlds/` tree and shrub GLBs were generated by that project's vegetation pipeline and retain exact source hashes and export metadata in their adjacent `manifest.json`. `assets/textures/sky/concept-b-day.webp` and `concept-b-clouds.webp` were transferred from the same clean revision; their SHA-256 hashes are `12e3bc912a9da40d955fef340ec635b00f5f3406ca65cd91ebbff4cd7cd1ee92` and `1a182444e4b42a71e8212419f5bfdb59e6e6f96d470baf9ed9ec2c2e2e830d0d` respectively.

`assets/textures/sky/sudomer-painted-day.webp` was generated with OpenAI image generation for this project, then encoded as WebP at quality 92 (1774 by 887 pixels). Brief: softly painted blue daylight, ivory clouds, pale atmospheric lower hemisphere, no land, sun disc, text or objects. SHA-256: `afe44acbeff37ddf7a45122c045b22baa3d2c2ac7f64a794b9232183861410fe`. The borrowed cloud skies remain available as source assets; this generated sky is now the default. Light and fog colors have been adjusted to this daylight palette.

Meadow details, worn road, soil profile and periodic pond normal texture are project-local procedural geometry/data, not downloaded assets.

## Campaign integration, 21 September 2026

The deterministic terrain-region field, campaign view adapter and terrain-derived scenery are project-local code. They consume the original campaign's live hex map and do not copy rules or scenario data into the renderer. Existing procedural-worlds-derived render, lighting, sky, focus and vegetation assets retain the provenance above.
